# <h1>GymCue</h1>
<h3>Less guesswork. More time for your workout.</h3>

<b>GymCue</b> helps you figure out the equipment at your gym and put together your workout. Browse exercises by muscle group or workout style, follow a beginner-friendly plan, or save your own. You can easily switch exercises when a machine is busy, log your lift weight and reps, and it even keeps Spotify within reach. 
<br>Oh, and your gym isn’t listed? Add it and its equipment to help the next person out.

With <b>GymCue</b> you can:
<br> - Find your gym and see its equipment. If it isn’t listed, add your gym and its setup to help the next person get started.
<br> - Browse exercises by muscle group or workout style. Find movements that fit your goals and the way you want to train.
<br> - Follow beginner-friendly guided workouts. Watch videos directly in the app to see how to perform each exercise.
<br> - Build workouts around your routine. Save them for your next session or share them with others.
<br> - Swap exercises when a machine is taken. Find an alternative and keep your workout moving.
<br> - Track your weight, reps, and progress. Record what you lift and see how your performance changes over time.
<br> - Control Spotify without leaving the app. Manage your music without switching away from your workout.

With <b>GymCue</b> there is less guessing & more lifting.
